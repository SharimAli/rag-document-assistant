WigaTech Employee Handbook (Sample Document)

1. Working Hours
Employees are expected to work Monday to Friday, 10:00 AM to 6:00 PM, with a
one-hour lunch break. On-site attendance is required for this role, at the
Gulshan-e-Iqbal Block 2 office in Karachi.

2. Leave Policy
Employees are entitled to 14 paid leave days per year, plus public holidays.
Sick leave beyond 2 consecutive days requires a medical certificate. Leave
requests should be submitted at least 3 working days in advance, except for
emergencies.

3. Probation Period
New hires undergo a 3-month probation period. Performance is reviewed at the
end of month 1, month 2, and month 3. Based on performance, an employee may
move from Intern to Junior Developer, and eventually to Mid-Level Developer.

4. Equipment
The company provides a laptop and any software licenses needed for AI
development work, including API credits for approved LLM providers used on
client projects.

5. Code of Conduct
All client data, prompts, and documents used in AI/RAG projects are
confidential and must not be shared outside the company or stored in
personal accounts.
